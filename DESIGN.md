# Design

The supplied peach, beige and warm gold assets define the visual identity. Extend it with warm ivory surfaces, brown text and generous spacing. Arabic type: Noto Sans Arabic. Mode: Persuade with familiar shopping controls.

First viewport: compact logo/navigation, original landscape campaign banner without cropping, understated manual slide controls. The mobile banner retains all artwork and moves the CTA beneath it.

Tokens: paper #fffdf9; cream #f6f0e8; ink #34251d; muted #786557; accent #86552f; line #e9dfd3; peach #f2d3bd. Content width 1180px. Corners 6px for buttons, 12–16px for visual sections. Four product columns desktop, two mobile. Motion limited to image hover and toasts; reduced-motion supported.
